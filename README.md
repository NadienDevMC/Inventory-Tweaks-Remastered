## Inventory Tweaks Revived


Inventory Tweaks Revived

This is an unofficial port of the original version by JimeoWan.

Since the mod is licensed under MIT, I am allowed to fork and update it.

### What's this mod about?


This client mod allows you to easily manage your inventory, in both single player and multiplayer. It works out of the box, and will make you gain a massive amount of time!


- It automatically replaces your tools as they break, and item stacks once emptied
- It sorts your inventory and your chests in a single click/keystroke
- It provides lots of powerful shortcuts to move items around
- It allows you to customize entirely the way your inventory is sorted, thanks to clever configuration files


Warning: The mod can be blocked by certain servers using plugins such as NoCheatPlus.


### Documentation


**Installing the mod**


1. Make sure NeoForge is installed
2. Put the jar in your mods folder
3. Run the game!


Otherwise, everything is explained on the official website.


**Compatibility** Inventory Tweaks works with almost any mod, and even allows to sort the most popular mod containers. The main conflicting mod is Convenient Inventory, though there might be others. Note that servers can block InvTweaks from working correctly by using anti-cheat plugins. In the case of NoCheatPlus, "inventory.fastclick" and potentially "combined.improbable" have been reported to cause conflicts with the mod's rapid movement of items.

### What's new in this build


- **NeoForge 1.21.1 port** (Java 21), with the settings screens rebuilt, a normal key binding for sorting, optional server support through NeoForge payloads, and 24 languages.

 **Colored symbol sorting buttons:**
  - ↺ default order.
  - ☲ columns 
  - ☷  rows: each family (planks, logs, ingots…) stays together, with no gaps between groups.
  - ♛ N shape: left column, diagonal and right column.
  - ✎ settings.

- **Wide chests:** sorting works on chests with more than 9 columns, with or without scrolling.
- **Buttons on the inventory screens:** in the survival and creative inventories they appear in a column to the right of the panel; on chests and other containers they sit on the title row.
- **⇑ / ⇓ transfer arrows** on chests: ⇑ fills the container from your inventory and ⇓ empties it into your inventory. A plain click moves one stack of the items the destination already holds; Shift or Ctrl moves everything. The "Arrows use hotbar" option also lets them use the hotbar.
- **Built-in Mouse Tweaks** (can be turned off in the advanced settings): Shift + drag quick-moves every stack the cursor passes over; dragging with a stack on the cursor collects the same items; the mouse wheel over a stack moves one item to the other inventory (up) or brings one from the other inventory (down).
- **Updated item tree** with 1.21.1 ids and tags, plus tree files for dozens of mods.
- **Sophisticated Storage and Sophisticated Backpacks:** their own sort buttons and insert/extract arrows are replaced by this mod's; it sorts the storage slots only, never the upgrades.
- **Applied Energistics 2 and AE2 Wireless Terminal Library:** sorts only your inventory, never the network.
